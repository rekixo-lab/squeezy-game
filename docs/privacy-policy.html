Privacy Policy – Squeezy Game

Developer: Rekixo · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Squeezy Game ("the game") is an offline mobile game for Android, made by Rekixo ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play.

Short version

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or send your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. The app does not request the Internet permission and does not connect to any server.

Data saved on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file only on your own phone. It holds your unlocked level, your coin count and your sound/music settings. This file never leaves your device through the game. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your personal Google backup. Google manages that backup under its own privacy policy, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

Permissions

The game does not request any special Android permissions.

Third-party services

The game contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social login. Google Play may collect information when you download apps from the store, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the game collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled (for example, by adding online features), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before releasing that version.

Contact

For any question about this policy, email: squeezygame@rekixo.com
