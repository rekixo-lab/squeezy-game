Privacy Policy – Squeezy Game

Developer: Rekixo · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Squeezy Game ("the game") is a mobile game for Android, made by Rekixo ("we"). This page explains what information is used when you play, and why.

Short version

We (Rekixo) do not collect, store or sell any personal data ourselves, and we have no accounts, no logins and no in-app purchases. The game itself can be played fully offline. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. When the device is online, Google's ad software collects some device data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

After some levels, the game may show a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To do this, Google may collect and process the following data from your device:

  • Device or other identifiers: the Android advertising ID (and Google's app set ID).
  • IP address: which may be used to estimate the general location of the device (not precise GPS location).
  • App interactions: for example app launches, taps and video views, including interactions with ads.
  • Diagnostic information: performance data of the app and the ad software, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Google uses this data for advertising (including personalised ads where you allowed it), ad measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. The data is sent to Google over an encrypted (TLS) connection. Rekixo does not receive your advertising ID or any personal data from these ads. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences (maximum rating PG).

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · About AdMob ads.

Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions where required): when you first open the game, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether ads may use your data for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options (shown where it is required).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Playing without ads data: if your device is offline, no ads are loaded and no data is sent; the game works normally.
  • You can also manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

Data saved on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file only on your own phone. It holds your unlocked level, your coin count and your sound/music settings. This file never leaves your device through the game. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your personal Google backup, which Google manages under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

Permissions

The game does not ask you for any runtime permission (no camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location prompts). The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds standard install-time permissions it needs to work: internet and network state access, the advertising ID permission, and system permissions for background work scheduling.

Third-party services

The only third-party service in the game is Google AdMob (with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent). There are no analytics, social login or other tracking SDKs. Google Play may collect information when you download apps from the store, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are restricted to a maximum content rating of PG.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before releasing that version.
